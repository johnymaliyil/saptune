FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does AI make SAP development cheaper?

AI speeds up the time-consuming parts — drafting specs, generating code, writing test cases and documentation. Our consultants then review and refine everything, so you pay for expert outcomes rather than repetitive hours.

Is AI-generated code safe for my production system?

Nothing reaches your system without human review. Every development is checked by an experienced SAP developer, tested and documented, and follows your transport and change-management process.

Do you work on ECC and S/4HANA?

Yes — we support SAP ECC (including IS-Retail) and S/4HANA, on-premise and cloud, plus SAP CAR, GTS, PI/PO, Integration Suite and BTP — including RAP and ABAP Cloud development.

Do you need access to our SAP system?

It depends on the work. We can deliver code and specs for your team to import, or work directly in your development system under your security policies and an NDA.

Do you only work on SAP?

No. Besides SAP we build cloud web apps, mobile apps, AI agents and chatbots, automations and dashboards for micro businesses, small and mid-size companies and enterprises, on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Cloudflare or SAP BTP.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a few days of agreeing scope. Send us your requirement and we'll come back with a quote quickly.
